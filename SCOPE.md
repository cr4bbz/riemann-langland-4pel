# Research scope

The repository name records a long-term question, not a present result. Work is divided into
three layers whose claims must never be conflated.

## Layer A — Riemann hypothesis

Formalize the exact proposition and the available theorem surface around the Riemann zeta
function. This layer uses ordinary Lean/Mathlib mathematics and does not depend on 4PEL.

## Layer B — Correspondences

Record actual translations, equivalences, preservation theorems, and missing implications among
analytic, spectral, geometric, arithmetic, and positivity formulations. “Langlands-like” is a
research analogy here; it becomes a formal claim only when a checked translation theorem exists.

## Layer C — 4PEL

After Layers A and B contain a nontrivial theory network, test whether four-valued positive and
negative support can classify:

- unformalized or missing implications as gaps;
- derivations with incompatible assumptions as contextual conflicts;
- genuinely preserved positive and negative support as invariant gluts;
- claims stable under certified translations as bridge-stable knowledge.

4PEL is not used to change the truth value of the Riemann hypothesis or to accept contradictory
classical proofs. Its possible role is metatheoretic: describing the state and transport of
knowledge across explicitly formalized theories.
